MANDATORY SEMANTIC REVIEW - NOT AUTOMATICALLY WAIVED

Compare every candidate change against the target branch's actual
governance documents, approved decisions, architecture and policies.

REQUIRED DOMAINS:

1. SUPER OWNER:
- Preserve exclusive owner identity and authority.
- Verify server-side authorization for every privileged operation.
- Verify owner dashboard, command/control center and all sections.
- Verify member management, bans, moderation and role assignment.
- Verify economy, wallets, coins, store, entitlements and grants.
- Verify configuration, audit logs, security controls and recovery.
- UI visibility is never authorization.
- No ordinary member or administrator may impersonate or override owner.

2. AUDIO AND CALLS:
- Live voice recording maximum remains 60 seconds.
- Distinguish live recording from uploaded audio files.
- Verify all conversation and room types.
- Verify server and test enforcement.

3. MEDIA:
- Images 3 MiB.
- GIF 3 MiB, 20 seconds, 50 frames.
- Audio 5 MiB and 60 seconds.
- Original video 25 MiB.
- Safe derived video 15 MiB and up to 90 seconds.
- Owner-pinned PDF 20 MiB.
- Verify server-side entitlement for every Media Saver operation.
- Do not extend server retention.

4. RETENTION:
- Private temporary: full deletion after 4 hours from server-recorded activity.
- Normal: media 4 hours, text 7 days.
- Important: media 4 hours, text 30 days.
- Do not activate a live deletion lifecycle while the unresolved
  mode-selection/consent decision remains unresolved.

5. ECONOMY:
- Centralized economy engine.
- Atomic, server-authoritative, auditable, idempotent transactions.
- Owner grants, entitlements, store, subscriptions and wallet integrity.
- Never invent prices, quotas or economic policy.

6. SECURITY AND PLATFORM:
- Firestore and Storage rules, callable authorization, replay protection.
- Firebase project identity and existing iOS registration.
- Android signing identity and PWA private-data caching.
- No secrets, credentials or signing keys in source control.

7. COST AND PRODUCTION:
- No billing activation, paid resource creation, deployment or production
  mutation.
- No changes to main without separate owner approval.

A filename scan is not proof of semantic compatibility.
Each domain requires code-path and test evidence.
