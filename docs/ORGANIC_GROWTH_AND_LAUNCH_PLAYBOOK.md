# Mawdda Platform — Six-Month Organic Growth and Launch Playbook

**Approved marketing ceiling:** USD 0–100 per month  
**Base planning spend:** USD 300 total over six months  
**Audience:** balanced Europe and Arabic-speaking countries  
**Primary objective:** retained, real members and healthy communities — not vanity installs.

## 1. Positioning and launch promise

Position Mawdda as a welcoming Arabic-first social community with conversations, public rooms, member profiles and community interaction. Explain only features that have passed the relevant product acceptance checks. Do not advertise real-money payments, identity verification, biometric rewards, in-app voice/video calls, privacy add-ons, or other features while they remain disabled, incomplete or unaccepted.

The product promise should emphasize:
- A comfortable, responsive experience on mobile.
- Useful conversations and moderated public rooms.
- Personal profiles and clearly explained optional premium features.
- Transparent wallet/store rules and visible transaction history.
- Member privacy, safety controls and reporting.

## 2. Six-month operating calendar

### Month 1 — Controlled invitation launch
- Invite a small cohort of real testers across Europe and Arabic-speaking communities.
- Publish official profile pages and a clear landing-page explanation.
- Publish 2–3 short demonstration videos per week, showing only verified features.
- Collect onboarding friction, crash, message-delivery and retention feedback.
- Do not spend on broad acquisition before signup and first-session quality are verified.

### Month 2 — Organic discovery
- Publish recurring short clips: room highlights (with consent), feature explainers, profile tips and community stories.
- Build a small calendar of moderated public-room events.
- Test one or two small community partnerships.
- Spend only on a narrowly targeted experiment if the first cohort returns.

### Month 3 — Referral pilot
- Introduce referral invitations only after server-side abuse controls are verified.
- Track invite → verified signup → first meaningful interaction → D7 retention.
- Do not issue high-value coin rewards for unverified registrations, self-referrals, duplicate devices or suspicious account clusters.
- Prefer non-cash recognition or capped low-value benefits until fraud behavior is measured.

### Month 4 — Micro-creator experiments
- Work with a few small creators who serve relevant Arabic/European communities.
- Use trackable campaign links or codes.
- Pay for verified deliverables and measurable qualified visits, not promised install counts.
- Stop any campaign that produces low-quality signups or policy-violating traffic.

### Month 5 — Community retention
- Run a predictable weekly schedule of moderated rooms and member activities.
- Feature member-created content only with permission.
- Improve first-week onboarding, empty states, helpful notifications and return journeys.
- Measure D7 and D30 retention by acquisition source.

### Month 6 — Scale only proven channels
- Compare all sources by cost per verified member and cost per D30-retained member.
- Increase spend only on sources that produce real, returning members.
- Expand to the countries and languages showing the strongest retention and manageable support load.
- Keep the monthly cap at USD 100 unless the owner separately approves a higher budget.

## 3. Monthly budget template

This is a flexible ceiling, not a requirement to spend the entire amount.

| Month | Planned cap | Primary use |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | $30 | Small content promotion and launch materials |
| 2 | $40 | Targeted content tests and community outreach |
| 3 | $50 | Referral pilot assets and limited promotion |
| 4 | $50 | Micro-creator test |
| 5 | $60 | Best-performing content/community channel |
| 6 | $70 | Scale only proven campaigns |
| **Total** | **$300** | Six-month base plan |

If organic growth is healthy, keep spend lower. If the product is not retaining members, pause ads and fix the experience first.

## 4. Content system

Create reusable content formats:
1. 15–30 second feature demonstration.
2. “How to join a room” walkthrough.
3. A privacy/safety feature explainer.
4. A short community event invitation.
5. A member story or testimonial with explicit permission.
6. A behind-the-scenes development update that is honest about what is live and what is still being tested.

Use Arabic as the initial primary language and add English/subtitles for European reach. Keep claims factual and avoid implying endorsements, guaranteed rewards or guaranteed privacy outcomes that are not technically supported.

## 5. Acquisition and retention dashboard

Track weekly and by source:
- Landing-page visits and store-page visits.
- Installs and verified registrations.
- Completion of onboarding.
- First meaningful action (send a message, join a room or complete a profile).
- DAU, WAU and MAU.
- D1, D7 and D30 retention.
- Invite acceptance and referral abuse rate.
- Cost per verified signup.
- Cost per D7-retained and D30-retained member.
- Reports, blocks, moderation load, crashes and failed media sends.

Do not optimize campaigns for installs alone. A campaign is successful only when it brings members who complete onboarding and return.

## 6. Campaign stop/go rules

Pause a campaign when:
- It produces fake, duplicate or incentivized low-intent signups.
- D7 retention is materially worse than the organic cohort.
- Support or moderation demand exceeds the available response capacity.
- The promoted feature is not yet accepted for release.
- Acquisition cost cannot be justified by the available monetization model.

Continue or cautiously increase a campaign when:
- Verified signup quality is good.
- D7/D30 retention is stable or improving.
- The community can be moderated safely.
- Cost per retained member is understood and within the owner-approved ceiling.

## 7. Launch readiness dependencies

Before public promotion, verify:
- Signup, email verification and password recovery on real devices.
- Stable room/chat/media experience on mobile networks.
- Clear privacy notice and account deletion flow.
- Report/block/appeal and owner moderation operations.
- Support contact and incident response procedure.
- No unaccepted or disabled feature is presented as generally available.
- Analytics respect privacy and collect only what is necessary.

## 8. Approval boundary

This playbook does not authorize ad purchases, creator contracts, paid campaigns, referral coin issuance, catalog price changes or production feature activation. All spending and any rewards remain subject to explicit owner approval and fraud controls.
